About Frown
FROWN › About
Manifesto
Too many people walk around pretending to be some serious, grown up version of themselves,
even when they know deep down that's not who they actually are or want to be.
People deserve to be different, to actually be themselves,
instead of whatever everyone else wants from them.
What's the point of living if you don't even get to enjoy it?
I'm not going to make up any meaning to no piece,
whatever meaning it has is whatever you give it.
